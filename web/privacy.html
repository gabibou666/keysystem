Privacy Policy

How AUDIT HUB protects and respects your personal data

Last updated: October 3, 2026 · Operator: AUDIT HUB

1. Commitment to Privacy & Data Minimization

At AUDIT HUB, we prioritize your privacy and strictly follow the principle of data minimization (Article 5.1.c of the General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR). We only collect and process the minimum amount of technical data strictly necessary to deliver, protect, and maintain our services.

2. Data Collected & Processing Purposes

Here is the exhaustive list of data processed through our platform:

Developer Workspace Data

Developer accounts store a display name and an internal account ID. Google and Discord sign-ins store the provider and its account identifier. Email accounts store the normalized email address, verification status and a password hash; plaintext passwords are not retained. Email verification and password recovery messages are sent through Resend when configured. Social identities are not automatically merged by email address.

Workspaces store project settings, license hashes and prefixes, license expiry and revocation state, and device identifiers hashed within each project. Uploaded Lua source and checkpoint provider tokens are encrypted at rest. Full manually issued license keys and project API tokens are shown once; their hashes are retained for validation.

Project validation history records the executor name, result and time, and is removed after 90 days by the daily cleanup. Checkpoint sessions use a browser-specific cookie lasting 30 minutes. Completed checkpoint keys are encrypted for recovery by the originating browser during that session; session records and task receipts are removed by the daily cleanup after one day. Developer account, project, script and license records are retained to operate the workspace, including expired or revoked licenses.

Developer Discord sign-in requests identity access only. Google sign-in requests basic profile and email access. Essential developer session cookies last up to seven days; OAuth state cookies last ten minutes. Verification and password-reset tokens expire after one hour and are stored hashed. Expired sessions and email tokens are removed by daily cleanup. Signing out revokes the current session; resetting a password revokes all sessions for that account. Developers configure their own checkpoint provider accounts and are responsible for how they distribute and manage project access.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To maintain a free and accessible service, AUDIT HUB partners with third-party advertising networks (such as HighRevenueFormat) and monetization partners (LootLabs).

In full accordance with European regulations and CNIL guidelines:

  • No advertising tracker is loaded without your prior, explicit consent.
  • You can refuse all non-essential trackers at any time with equal ease.
  • Third-party providers operate under their own independent privacy policies and data governance standards.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: You can request confirmation of whether personal data concerning you is being processed.
  • Right to rectification: You can request the correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("Right to be forgotten"): You can request the deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to restriction and objection: You can object to or limit the processing of your data.
  • Right to withdraw consent: You can withdraw your cookie consent at any moment via our Cookie Settings tool.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via our official Discord community server or contact our team through the AUDIT HUB platform.

5. Security Measures

AUDIT HUB employs state-of-the-art security measures to protect your information, including HTTPS / TLS encryption, SHA-256 one-way cryptographic hashing for IP addresses, automated jail and rate-limiting anti-DDoS defenses, and secure session management.
