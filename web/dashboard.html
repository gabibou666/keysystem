    YOUR PERSONAL DATA
    Your account. Your control.
    Download your workspace data or permanently close your account.
    Take your data with you
    A JSON copy of your profile, linked identities, projects, licenses and activity. Passwords, private credentials and other users’ personal data are excluded.
    Close your workspace
    Delete your account and its projects, scripts, licenses and personal data from the active database. All access issued by your projects will stop.
    Read the privacy policy for retention details.
    

    Create a project
    A project groups one hosted script, its licenses and its checkpoint configuration. It starts with 24-hour licenses and device binding enabled; you can change these defaults in Project settings.
    

    Issue licenses
    Each key gives access to this project only. Save the generated keys and send one to each user.
    

    Save your keys
    Copy or download this value before closing. It will not be displayed again.
    

    Permanently delete your account?
    This removes your workspace, public profile, hosted scripts and licenses. It signs you out on every device. Export your data first if you want to keep a copy.
    Copies already downloaded by others and provider or hosting backups are covered by their own retention rules. Moderation audit references are anonymized; the separate security log retains staff actor, target and IP details.
