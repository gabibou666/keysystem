Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and respect your privacy choices

Last updated: September 19, 2026 · Operator: AUDIT HUB

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies and local storage are small text files or data fragments stored on your device when visiting a website. They allow the site to remember your preferences, ensure technical security, and facilitate service operation.

2. Cookies Used on AUDIT HUB

We classify cookies and storage technologies into two distinct categories:

Category A: Strictly Necessary Cookies (Exempt from Consent)

These cookies are vital for the technical functioning, session security, and anti-abuse safeguards of the website.

Name / Key Provider Purpose Duration
ks_cookie_consent AUDIT HUB Stores your cookie consent preferences (Accept / Reject) 6 months (localStorage)
session_token AUDIT HUB Admin session authentication via Discord OAuth2 Session / 24 hours
__cf_bm / cf_clearance Cloudflare Anti-DDoS, rate-limiting, and bot defense protection 30 minutes to 1 year

Category B: Advertising & Monetization Trackers (Requires Consent)

To maintain our key delivery system free of charge, we partner with advertising networks. In strict compliance with CNIL recommendations (Deliberations 2020-091 and 2020-092) and the GDPR:

  • No advertising scripts or trackers are loaded until you have clicked "Accept All" or opted in.
  • Refusing advertising cookies does not prevent you from obtaining a key through our standard verification steps.
  • You can withdraw or update your consent at any time.
