Cookies used by the platform

This site uses cookies for developer sign-in, OAuth security, registration limits and checkpoint recovery. It does not load the old advertising networks, advertising consent manager or anti-adblock gate.

  • ah_session: developer session, up to seven days. Signing out revokes the session.
  • ah_registration: signed registration-limit identifier, up to 180 days.
  • ah_oauth_state and ah_google: browser-bound OAuth state, ten minutes.
  • ah_checkpoint_* and ah_checkpoint_current_*: checkpoint session and recovery in the same browser, thirty minutes.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking these cookies can prevent sign-in or checkpoint recovery. Checkpoint providers operate on their own sites and may use additional cookies under their own policies.
