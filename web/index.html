Frequently asked

Which executors are supported?

Synapse Z, Wave, Xeno, Delta, Codex, Fluxus, Hydrogen, Solara, Ronin, MacSploit, Potassium and most others. The loader auto-detects your executor and adapts its UI. If yours has no HTTP function, you'll get a clear notification instead of a silent failure.

How long does a key last?

12 hours (1 ad) or 24 hours (2 ads) — your choice when getting it. When it expires, you don't need a new key: renewing re-activates the same key, so your loader still has it saved.

Can I use my key on another Roblox account?

No — the first time you use a key, it binds to your Roblox account. If someone shares their key, it gets rejected for anyone else and the owner is traceable (shared keys get auto-revoked after 3 alerts from different accounts/IPs).

My key says "expired" — what do I do?

Go to Get a key and click 12h or 24h again: it renews your existing key, same string, no need to re-paste it in your executor (one ad for 12h, two for 24h). The countdown on the home page shows exactly how much time is left.

Why do I need to sign in with Discord?

One sign-in, and you're added to our server automatically. It links your key to your Discord account — this is what stops people from farming keys with bots and proxies (max 2 ad sessions / 12h per IP, 4 per Discord account).

The ad page doesn't credit my key?

Complete the tasks all the way through — the key page checks automatically every 2 seconds and will pop it up the moment the ad network confirms. If you closed the tab, just reopen Get a key: your session resumes by itself. Also make sure your ad blocker is disabled on this site — it blocks key delivery.
